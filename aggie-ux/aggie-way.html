Leadership in everyday behavior.

Five proposed commitments. Open one to see examples for yourself, your team and the university.

Lead with valuesUse our values to guide decisions.
  • In your own work: Use values to explain a decision and own its effect.
  • With people and teams: Explain expectations, act consistently and acknowledge mistakes.
  • Across the institution: Make tradeoffs and decision rights visible; model accountability.

Related Core Values: Integrity · Leadership

Serve othersListen, respond and remove obstacles.
  • In your own work: Listen to the person behind the request and follow through.
  • With people and teams: Remove obstacles while helping people build their own capability.
  • Across the institution: Include affected groups and consider the university-wide impact.

Related Core Values: Selfless Service · Respect

Develop peopleHelp people build confidence and capability.
  • In your own work: Share a useful skill and invite feedback.
  • With people and teams: Hold development conversations and create supported assignments.
  • Across the institution: Mentor, sponsor development and build future leadership capacity.

Related Core Values: Leadership · Loyalty

Deliver resultsSet clear expectations and learn from outcomes.
  • In your own work: Set a clear goal, check quality and learn from the outcome.
  • With people and teams: Align priorities, provide feedback and address unmet expectations.
  • Across the institution: Use evidence to guide strategy, resources and improvement.

Related Core Values: Excellence · Integrity

Strengthen Texas A&MConnect people and resources to our shared purpose.
  • In your own work: Use time and resources responsibly; connect your work to purpose.
  • With people and teams: Collaborate across functions and build a respectful team culture.
  • Across the institution: Steward the institution, develop partnerships and improve shared systems.

Related Core Values: Loyalty · Excellence · Selfless Service

12 guiding principles.

Summaries of the president’s published principles, in their original order.

01. Lead steadily through our values

Let Aggie values guide calm, consistent decisions.

02. Build a supportive culture

Make the workplace positive and respectful.

03. Bring effort and excellence

Work diligently and aim for high standards.

04. Make expectations clear

Establish responsibilities and follow through on accountability.

05. Help others develop

Support the growth of people around you.

06. Connect across A&M

Align and collaborate across the university and System.

07. Make work easier

Remove obstacles that slow useful work.

08. Communicate early

Keep people informed so surprises are avoided.

09. Care for our resources

Use resources efficiently and with sound stewardship.

10. Own the outcome

Accept responsibility, acknowledge mistakes and learn.

11. Adapt with purpose

Respond flexibly as needs and circumstances change.

12. Keep improving

Pursue better approaches and make room for innovation.

Core Values in everyday moments
  • Excellence: Check the details, seek feedback and improve the next attempt.
  • Integrity: Keep a commitment. If circumstances change, explain what happened and reset expectations.
  • Leadership: Name a shared goal and help the team take its next step.
  • Loyalty: Share knowledge across teams and follow through when a colleague needs support.
  • Respect: Invite another perspective, listen fully and acknowledge the person behind the work.
  • Selfless Service: Help solve a problem because it matters to someone else.
What you do each day can help someone go further.

A colleague needs a clear answer. A new employee needs a welcome. A team needs someone to listen, make a decision or carry an idea forward. These are everyday opportunities to lead.

Whether you teach, advise, conduct research, support operations or supervise a team, your work connects to people who count on Texas A&M. Leading the Aggie way means considering those people, taking responsibility and helping others succeed.
