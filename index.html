A shared purpose

What you do each day
can help someone go further.

A colleague needs a clear answer. A new employee needs a welcome. A team needs someone to listen, make a decision or carry an idea forward. These are everyday opportunities to lead.

Whether you teach, advise, conduct research, support operations or supervise a team, your work connects to people who count on Texas A&M. Leading the Aggie way means considering those people, taking responsibility and helping others succeed.

Our university purpose

“To develop leaders of character dedicated to serving the greater good.”

What we stand for

Six values.
One shared commitment.

Our Core Values give us a common foundation. We bring them to life in our decisions, our relationships and the way we serve.

Read the university’s Core Values

Excellence

Bring care and high standards to the work.

In your workCheck the details, seek feedback and improve the next attempt.

Integrity

Be honest, dependable and accountable.

In your workKeep a commitment. If circumstances change, explain what happened and reset expectations.

Leadership

Set an example that helps others move forward.

In your workName a shared goal and help the team take its next step.

Loyalty

Invest in the university and the people around you.

In your workShare knowledge across teams and follow through when a colleague needs support.

Respect

Treat people with dignity, care and fairness.

In your workInvite another perspective, listen fully and acknowledge the person behind the work.

Selfless Service

Contribute to something beyond yourself.

In your workHelp solve a problem because it matters to someone else.

President Susan Ballabina

12 guiding principles.
A direction for how we lead.

The president’s principles connect leadership with culture, accountability and service across Texas A&M.

Read the president’s official wording

Concise summaries, presented in the president’s published order.

  1. 01

    Lead steadily through our values

    Let Aggie values guide calm, consistent decisions.

  2. 02

    Build a supportive culture

    Make the workplace positive and respectful.

  3. 03

    Bring effort and excellence

    Work diligently and aim for high standards.

  4. 04

    Make expectations clear

    Establish responsibilities and follow through on accountability.

  5. 05

    Help others develop

    Support the growth of people around you.

  6. 06

    Connect across A&M

    Align and collaborate across the university and System.

  7. 07

    Make work easier

    Remove obstacles that slow useful work.

  8. 08

    Communicate early

    Keep people informed so surprises are avoided.

  9. 09

    Care for our resources

    Use resources efficiently and with sound stewardship.

  10. 10

    Own the outcome

    Accept responsibility, acknowledge mistakes and learn.

  11. 11

    Adapt with purpose

    Respond flexibly as needs and circumstances change.

  12. 12

    Keep improving

    Pursue better approaches and make room for innovation.
