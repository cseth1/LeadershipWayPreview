Before you buckle in

Quick answers.

Who can race?

Everyone in HROE. Any role, any team, any level. Crews mix people from different teams, so whatever you know from your daily work is exactly what a crew needs.

What will we work on?

Every crew works on the same HROE strategic commitment. Everyone finds out what it is on race day, January 12, along with the first clue.

How does this connect to Beyond 150?

Beyond 150 is the university's direction: one vision and twelve strategic imperatives. HROE carries Imperative 11, a vision-driven and thriving workplace. The commitment every crew races on comes from that same map, so the ideas you bring move HROE and Texas A&M in the same direction.

Do I need to know about the TV show?

Not at all. We borrow the fun parts: crews, clues and a finish line. Everything you need to know is on this page.

Can I bring my own crew?

Yes, as long as it mixes teams. Build your own crew of 4 to 8 and submit it in the crew form. No crew yet? Pick that option in the form and we'll place you on one.

How much time does it take?

Seven weeks, one short leg at a time, with feedback along the way. The expected weekly time will be shared before crews lock in December.

Does anyone get eliminated?

No. Every crew finishes, every crew's ideas get reviewed, and every finisher gets recognized. The top three crews take the podium, and the winning crew earns a paid professional development course.
